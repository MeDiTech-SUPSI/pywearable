Visualization

Matplotlib visualizations for wearable trajectories and monitoring data.

Most functions consume the canonical trajectory DataFrames returned by the domain modules. Loader-aware composite views are also provided when a useful figure must combine multiple streams, such as sleep stages, heart rate, activity, or adherence. Every public function can display and save its figure and returns its axes for further customization.

pywearable.visualization.plot_adherence_summary(loader, *, metric='heart_rate', user_id='all', follow_up_days=None, worn_day_threshold=0.7, include_without_evidence=False, title=None, figsize=None, axes=None, show=True, save_to=None)

Summarize aligned cohort adherence and nightly availability.

Every participant is aligned to their first date with primary-stream or usable-sleep evidence. The default common follow-up is the longest observed trajectory among the requested participants. Shorter trajectories contribute trailing non-adherence to that shared denominator.

Parameters:

  • loader – Loader used by pywearable.adherence.get_adherence().

  • metric (str) – "heart_rate" or "bbi". Sleep has no hourly stream and is not suitable as the primary wearing-time stream.

  • user_id – One participant, several participants, "all", or None.

  • follow_up_days (int | None) – Shared number of participant-relative days to display. With None, use the maximum first-to-last evidence span across requested participants. A final partial study week is retained.

  • worn_day_threshold (float) – Daily primary-stream adherence required for a day to count as worn.

  • include_without_evidence (bool) – Include requested participants with no primary or sleep evidence. They are aligned to the earliest inferred cohort start. By default they are excluded because their individual study start cannot be inferred.

  • title (str | None) – Figure title and optional size. Height grows with cohort size.

  • figsize – Figure title and optional size. Height grows with cohort size.

  • axes (Mapping | None) – Optional mapping with axes named wearing_days, available_nights, weekly_wearing, weekly_nights, weekday_pattern, and intraday.

  • show (bool) – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

  • save_to – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

Returns:

Mapping of the six panel names to Matplotlib axes.

Return type:

dict

Notes

Participant-week heatmaps use the exact integer range 0–7. Grey cells mean that a stream is unsupported; zero qualifying days or nights is red. The final partial week can contain fewer than seven requested days. Weekday and intraday panels average raw primary-stream adherence.

Rows are calendar dates and columns are local time-of-day intervals. This plot shows the coverage semantics already present in the trajectory; it does not infer wearing time itself.

  • range_label (str | None) – Optional legend labels. Defaults describe the statistic and requested resolutions.

pywearable.visualization.plot_period_comparison(trajectory, feature, *, user_id=None, kind='bar', show_percent_change=True, favorable_direction=None, period_format='%Y-%m-%d', title=None, xlabel=None, ylabel=None, figsize=(11, 5), ax=None, show=True, save_to=None)

Compare already-aggregated periods without re-aggregating the data.

Values use one neutral color. The only annotations are signed consecutive percentage changes; absolute values remain readable from the y-axis. Missing periods are blank and break the comparison chain.

Parameters:

  • trajectory (DataFrame) – Canonical trajectory already calculated at the comparison resolution, such as weekly total steps or monthly mean resting heart rate.

  • feature (str) – Numeric period-level feature to draw.

  • user_id – Participant to draw. It may be omitted for a single-user trajectory.

  • kind (str) – "bar" or "line".

  • show_percent_change (bool) – Annotate change from the immediately preceding non-missing period. A missing period breaks the comparison chain.

  • favorable_direction (str | None) – "higher" colors increases green and decreases vermillion; "lower" reverses that interpretation. With None, green means an increase and vermillion a decrease, without implying benefit.

  • period_format (str) – strftime format used for the x-axis labels.

  • title (str | None) – Labels and layout for a new or supplied axis.

  • xlabel (str | None) – Labels and layout for a new or supplied axis.

  • ylabel (str | None) – Labels and layout for a new or supplied axis.

  • figsize – Labels and layout for a new or supplied axis.

  • ax (Axes | None) – Labels and layout for a new or supplied axis.

  • show (bool) – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

  • save_to – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

Notes

This function does not choose or calculate the period aggregation. Use the relevant domain hierarchy first so the meaning of each bar is explicit.

Returns:

The period-comparison axis.

pywearable.visualization.plot_sleep_summary(loader, user_id, start_date=None, end_date=None, *, show_scores=True, show_boundaries=True, cpd=None, cpd_trend=False, cpd_trend_kwargs=None, title=None, figsize=None, fontsize=12, axes=None, show=True, save_to=None)

Plot compact native hypnograms for a participant across many nights.

Unlike the generic trajectory plots, this view accepts a loader because it combines sleep summaries, native stage intervals, calculated average sleep timing, provider scores, and optional composite-phase-deviation (CPD) panels.

Parameters:

  • loader – Loader exposing canonical sleep summaries and sleep-stage intervals.

  • user_id – Participant identifier.

  • start_date – Inclusive sleep-calendar-date bounds. Missing dates remain visible as empty rows. A night assigned to start_date retains stages from the preceding civil date.

  • end_date – Inclusive sleep-calendar-date bounds. Missing dates remain visible as empty rows. A night assigned to start_date retains stages from the preceding civil date.

  • show_scores (bool) – Write the provider’s nightly sleep score to the right of each row when available. Scores are colored using fixed quality bands.

  • show_boundaries (bool) – Draw dotted vertical lines at the participant’s circular mean bedtime and wake-up time. It does not add a marker for every night.

  • cpd – Optional "midpoint", "duration", or sequence containing both. Each adds one deliberately narrow panel beside the hypnograms.

  • cpd_trend (bool) – Add a historical baseline and normal range to each CPD panel.

  • cpd_trend_kwargs (Mapping | None) – Keyword arguments forwarded to pywearable.trajectory.compute_trajectory_trend() for CPD panels.

  • title (str | None) – Figure title and optional size. The default grows vertically with the number of requested nights. fontsize controls titles, labels, ticks, scores, and the shared legend.

  • figsize – Figure title and optional size. The default grows vertically with the number of requested nights. fontsize controls titles, labels, ticks, scores, and the shared legend.

  • fontsize (float) – Figure title and optional size. The default grows vertically with the number of requested nights. fontsize controls titles, labels, ticks, scores, and the shared legend.

  • axes – Existing axes as a mapping keyed by "sleep" and requested CPD feature names, or as an ordered sequence.

  • show (bool) – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

  • save_to – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

Returns:

Axes keyed by "sleep" and any requested CPD feature names.

Return type:

dict

Notes

The clock axis is cropped to the observed sleep range rather than showing an entire 24-hour day.

pywearable.visualization.plot_weekly_profile(trajectory, feature, *, user_id=None, loader=None, aggregation='mean', kind='lines', interval=None, include_imputed=False, cmap='viridis', night_window='auto', night_color='#C9D7EC', title=None, ylabel=None, figsize=(10, 9), ax=None, show=True, save_to=None)

Plot a participant’s typical within-day profile for each weekday.

This descriptive view aggregates an already prepared subdaily trajectory by weekday and local-time slot. It answers questions such as when steps are usually accumulated or how heart rate varies through a typical weekday. It does not calculate calendar-period features.

Parameters:

  • trajectory (DataFrame) – Canonical subdaily trajectory indexed by (user_id, time).

  • feature (str) – Numeric feature summarized within each weekday/time-of-day cell.

  • user_id – Participant to draw. It may be omitted for a single-user trajectory.

  • loader – Optional loader used when night_window="auto". Each observed sleep session is expressed relative to its sleep calendar date, averaged by that weekday, and only then split at local midnight. Consequently the evening segment on one panel and morning segment on the next panel are two parts of the same mean night.

  • aggregation (str) – "mean" or "median" across matching weekday/slot values.

  • kind (str) – "lines" (default) creates seven aligned small-multiple line plots. "heatmap" creates a compact weekday-by-time matrix.

  • interval – Time-of-day bin width accepted by pandas.Timedelta. By default the most common native spacing is retained. This parameter aggregates existing rows; it does not reconstruct finer observations.

  • include_imputed (bool) – Include values marked by <feature>__is_imputed in the summary.

  • cmap (str | Colormap) – Colormap used only for kind="heatmap".

  • night_window – "auto" (default) uses weekday-specific local-day sleep segments when a loader is supplied. An explicit (start, end) clock pair shades the same interval in every panel. A window crossing midnight is supported; pass None to disable shading.

  • night_color – Color of the night shading.

  • title (str | None) – Labels and layout. For line plots, ax must contain seven axes; for a heatmap it must be one axis.

  • ylabel (str | None) – Labels and layout. For line plots, ax must contain seven axes; for a heatmap it must be one axis.

  • figsize – Labels and layout. For line plots, ax must contain seven axes; for a heatmap it must be one axis.

  • ax – Labels and layout. For line plots, ax must contain seven axes; for a heatmap it must be one axis.

  • show (bool) – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

  • save_to – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

Returns:

Seven axes for line small multiples, or one heatmap axis.

Return type:

numpy.ndarray or matplotlib.axes.Axes

pywearable.visualization.plot_weekly_overview(loader, user_id, start_date, end_date=None, *, start_hour=12, heart_rate_resolution='5min', activity_resolution='15min', missing_threshold=0.0, title=None, figsize=None, axes=None, show=True, save_to=None)

Plot sleep stages, steps, heart rate, and missingness day by day.

Each row is one 24-hour participant-local window. Windows begin at start_hour so a noon-to-noon layout keeps the night’s sleep together. This loader-aware composition intentionally performs the required domain loading because the useful view depends on three synchronized streams.

Parameters:

  • loader – Loader exposing timestamped activity samples, heart-rate samples, and sleep-stage intervals. Loaders with daily-only activity summaries cannot produce this subdaily view.

  • user_id – Participant identifier.

  • start_date – Calendar labels for the first and exclusive final row. If end_date is omitted, seven rows are shown.

  • end_date – Calendar labels for the first and exclusive final row. If end_date is omitted, seven rows are shown.

  • start_hour (int) – Local hour at the left edge of every 24-hour row, from 0 through 23.

  • heart_rate_resolution – Fixed aggregation windows passed to the corresponding domain APIs. Activity bars are totals per activity_resolution window, which is stated explicitly on the activity y-axis.

  • activity_resolution – Fixed aggregation windows passed to the corresponding domain APIs. Activity bars are totals per activity_resolution window, which is stated explicitly on the activity y-axis.

  • missing_threshold (float) – A window is shaded grey when neither heart-rate nor activity coverage is greater than this value. Positive steps can therefore support a window even if heart rate is absent.

  • title (str | None) – Optional figure title and size. No title is added when title is None. Height grows with the number of rows.

  • figsize – Optional figure title and size. No title is added when title is None. Height grows with the number of rows.

  • axes – Optional sequence containing one primary axis per day. Heart-rate twin axes are created by the function.

  • show (bool) – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

  • save_to – Display the figure and/or save it to a path. Saving does not close it.

Returns:

{"activity": axes, "heart_rate": twin_axes}.

Return type:

dict

Notes

Sleep stages are the blue stepped hypnogram near the top of each row. Grey spans are explicit intervals without either supported stream, not periods of zero movement. Steps are dark bars and heart rate is a vermillion line.
