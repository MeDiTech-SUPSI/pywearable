Cycles

Define sleep-based intervals without calculating domain features.

pywearable.cycle.get_intervals(loader, cycle_type, user_id='all', start_date=None, end_date=None, missing_end='invalidate')

Return expected local-time rows for one requested cycle type.

cycle_night is the provider main-sleep session. cycle_awake runs from its getup/session end to the following night’s bedtime, and cycle_physiological runs from one bedtime to the following bedtime. Missing following nights invalidate the latter two by default. Setting missing_end='midnight' closes a cycle with a known start at the next local midnight; it never invents a missing starting boundary. Naps remain inside the latter two cycles and do not alter their boundaries. Date bounds select cycle calendar_date labels; selected intervals retain their complete physical boundaries and are never clipped.

Parameters:

  • loader (BaseLoader) – Loader exposing canonical sleep summaries or sessions.

  • cycle_type (str) – One of cycle_night, cycle_awake, or cycle_physiological.

  • user_id (Union[str, list, None]) – One participant, several participants, or "all"/None.

  • start_date – Half-open range of participant-local sleep calendar labels. Selected cycles retain their complete physical intervals.

  • end_date – Half-open range of participant-local sleep calendar labels. Selected cycles retain their complete physical intervals.

  • missing_end (str) – Policy for an awake or physiological cycle whose following bedtime is unavailable. "invalidate" leaves it invalid; "midnight" uses the next local midnight when the starting boundary is known.

Returns:

A (user_id, calendar_date) frame with start_time, end_time, interval_valid, and end_source columns.

Return type:

pandas.DataFrame

Raises:

ValueError – If cycle_type or missing_end is unsupported.
