Tesserae

The public Tesserae dataset [1] can be read directly from its published CSV layout:

from pywearable.loader import TesseraeCSVLoader

loader = TesseraeCSVLoader("path/to/tesserae-csv")

For repeatedly analysing a local conversion, use the Parquet loader and install the parquet optional dependency:

from pywearable.loader import TesseraeParquetLoader

loader = TesseraeParquetLoader("path/to/tesserae-parquet")

Both loaders expose canonical timestamped activity and heart-rate samples. The Parquet loader also exposes BBI data when present.

See pywearable.loader.TesseraeCSVLoader and pywearable.loader.TesseraeParquetLoader for their APIs.

References

[1]

Mattingly, Stephen M., et al. “The Tesserae Project: Large-Scale, Longitudinal, In Situ, Multimodal Sensing of Information Workers.” Extended Abstracts of the 2019 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (2019).
